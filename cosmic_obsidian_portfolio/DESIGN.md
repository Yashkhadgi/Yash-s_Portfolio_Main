## Brand & Style

The design system projects the persona of an elite systems thinker, engineer, and digital architect. Tailored for venture capitalists, executive design directors, founders, and deep-tech collaborators, the visual environment crafts an evocative, cinematic "Builder's Universe." It departs from cookie-cutter developer portfolios by treating digital space as an interactive architectural cosmos.

The movement fuses **Editorial Glassmorphism** with **Technical Spatial Minimalism**:
- **Atmospheric Obsidian:** Deep, luminous space fields rather than flat black backdrops, invoking the quiet gravity of a planetary observatory.
- **Electric Soft Accents:** Bioluminescent lavender and low-frequency mint punctuate deliberate interaction nodes.
- **Architectural Editorial:** Poised high-contrast editorial typography balanced with sharp technical sans surfaces, evoking structural blueprints elevated to fine art.
- **Cinematic Tension:** Expansive spatial pauses, hyper-fine hairline borders, and atmospheric depth that make projects feel like discovered artifacts rather than static resume cards.

## Colors

The palette establishes an obsidian cosmic framework punctuated by luminous spectral emissions. 

- **Primary Canvas (`#0B0D11`) & Elevated Void (`#12151C`):** Form the bottom-tier cosmic plane. Surfaces never use pure `#000000`, preserving deep chromatic blue-gray undertones that absorb ambient colored glows.
- **Electric Lavender (`#B8A4F8`):** The primary kinetic signal. Represents conceptual elegance, intellectual focus, and active interactive states (magnetic cursors, primary buttons, focal highlights).
- **Muted Mint-Green (`#6EE7B7`):** The system telemetry tone. Represents living systems, operational deployments, metrics, code execution, and dynamic status pings.
- **Starlight Neutral Spectrum (`#F3F4F6`, `#E2E8F0`, `#94A3B8`, `#475569`):** Carefully calibrated off-whites and slate tones that prevent retinal fatigue while delivering high-contrast readability against obsidian backdrops.
- **Surface Translucency:** Frosted cards leverage `rgba(18, 21, 28, 0.65)` with sub-pixel borders tinted at `rgba(243, 244, 246, 0.08)` to reflect light like polished obsidian glass.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is grounded in high-contrast tension between editorial authority and engineering precision.

- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers dramatic grandeur and architectural elegance. Used for monumental project titles, opening thesis statements, and numerical section markers. Italics are reserved for poetic descriptors or secondary thematic nouns.
- **Body & Structural Text (`Geist`):** Delivers crisp, uncompromised legibility for narrative case studies, process reflections, and system breakdowns. Neutral, hyper-legible, and balanced.
- **Telemetry & Labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to metadata, status indicators, architecture specs, timeline milestones, and navigation breadcrumbs. Always rendered in upper-case with wide tracking (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) to establish an engineered field-guide aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an open architectural canvas. Rather than packing density, it treats empty space as dark cosmic matter that isolates and elevates key artifacts.

- **Grid Framework:** A 12-column adaptive fluid grid on desktop (`max-width: 1440px`), collapsing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Cinematic Rhythm:** Section transitions leverage expansive vertical intervals (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to establish rhythmic pacing between interactive artifacts and long-form narratives.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Hero compositions and flagship case study previews alternate column spans (e.g., 7 columns for media, 5 columns for narrative title) with baseline micro-staggering to break standard SaaS rigidity.
- **Responsive Stacking:** On mobile devices (`< 768px`), all multi-column compositions resolve into an intentional vertical sequence: Title Metadata (`label-caps`) → Headline (`headline-lg-mobile`) → Interactive Media Window → Deep Dive Body.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial depth is conveyed not through heavy drop shadows, but through **layered darkness, selective illumination, and atmospheric blurs**.

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Pure `#0B0D11` enhanced with subtle radial background gradients in `#12151C` and low-opacity spectral purple vignettes (`rgba(184, 164, 248, 0.03)`).
- **Surface Tiers (Glass Cards):** 
  - Background: `rgba(18, 21, 28, 0.60)` to `rgba(22, 27, 36, 0.75)`
  - Backing Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`
  - Outline: 1px continuous hairline border using `rgba(243, 244, 246, 0.07)`.
- **Cosmic Hover & Focal Illumination:**
  - Active elements project localized chromatic halos: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(184, 164, 248, 0.3), 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(184, 164, 248, 0.15), 0 0 60px -20px rgba(110, 231, 183, 0.1)`.
- **Deep Spatial Occlusion:** Floating panels, terminal overlays, and flyout navigation draw upon deep obsidian shadows: `0 30px 60px -12px rgba(0, 2, 6, 0.85)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined geometric balance (`roundedness: 2` / baseline `0.5rem`).

- **Geometry Rationale:** Avoids both cold brutalist sharp corners and overly playful pill-shapes for main containers. The balanced 8px (`0.5rem`) radius evokes milled dark slate, high-end optical instruments, and spacecraft instrumentation panels.
- **Interactive Micro-Elements:** Micro-badges, status pills, and button outlines utilize fully rounded pill radii (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to contrast cleanly against larger, structured glass cards.
- **Internal Nested Radii:** For inner media elements nested within a card with `rounded-lg` (16px), interior elements apply an 8px radius to preserve concentric spatial balance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Magnetic Primary:** Solid electric lavender fill (`#B8A4F8`) with obsidian typography (`#0B0D11`), font weight 600. Includes physical magnetic tethering on desktop hover. Elevation transitions with a soft lavender outer glow (`rgba(184, 164, 248, 0.35)`).
- **Ghost Obsidian:** Translucent background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), 1px border (`rgba(243, 244, 246, 0.12)`), text `#F3F4F6`. On hover, the border shifts to electric lavender (`#B8A4F8`) and surface shifts to `rgba(184, 164, 248, 0.08)`.
- **Action Links:** Typography in `JetBrains Mono` with trailing micro-arrow (`→`). Hover triggers a smooth 4px rightward translation with color transition from off-white to `#6EE7B7`.

### Cards & Artifact Panels
- **Flagship Project Card:** Frosted obsidian glass base (`#12151C`, 70% opacity, 16px blur). Hairline border with dynamic gradient angle tracking mouse position. Top label in `label-caps` (`#94A3B8`), Bodoni Moda title (`#F3F4F6`), and live project status badge.
- **Metric / Spec Card:** Ultra-minimalist container featuring top-bordered mint accent line (`1px solid #6EE7B7`), monospaced technical figures, and muted secondary labels.

### Chips & Status Indicators
- **Telemetry Pill:** Compact container (`px-2.5 py-1`), rounded-full, background `rgba(110, 231, 183, 0.08)`, border `rgba(110, 231, 183, 0.25)`, text `#6EE7B7` in `label-tag`.
- **Live State Beacon:** Includes an animated pulsing radial orb (`4px` diameter) preceding the label to denote active deployment or current availability.

### Form Inputs
- **Terminal Input Field:** Obsidian backdrop (`#0E1117`), inset hairline border (`rgba(243, 244, 246, 0.1)`), monospaced placeholder in `#475569`. On focus: border shifts cleanly to `#B8A4F8` with a zero-spread 1px ring and a faint radial ambient flare.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Precision Checkbox:** 16x16px square, 3px border radius. Unchecked: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` border. Checked: background `#B8A4F8`, border `#B8A4F8`, displaying a sharp obsidian checkmark icon.

### Interactive Cosmic Artifacts (Domain Specific)
- **Constellation Grid / Skill Matrix:** Monospaced interactive node network with connecting vector hairlines at 15% opacity, activating mint or lavender paths upon cursor proximity.
- **Milestone Log (Timeline):** Continuous vertical hairline in `#1E2430` flanked by monospaced timestamps and Bodoni Moda impact titles.

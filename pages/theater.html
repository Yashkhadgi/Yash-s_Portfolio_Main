Context & Mandate

In large-scale customer engagement workflows, customer support platforms drown under repetitive, low-context complaints. Support agents manually categorize issues, leading to operational bottlenecks, misassigned tickets, and delayed SLA resolution. CustomerPulse was designed to tackle this through state-of-the-art retrieval augmented intelligence.

THE BOTTLENECK

Unstructured Noise & High Variance

Standard keyword-matching systems fail when users describe the same underlying fault using completely disparate vocabularies. Furthermore, agents often draft inconsistent answers that conflict with product updates.

THE SOLUTION

Dense Vector Cosine Similarity & LangChain Loops

We established an end-to-end semantic embedding pipeline that normalizes vernacular text into dense mathematical representations. The pipeline maps new incoming complaints against historically validated fixes, synthesizing a verified solution draft instantly.
